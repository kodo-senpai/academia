Manual & Formulas

How To Use Academia OS

Master your attendance calculations, protect your hall ticket, and plan bunks responsibly.

01

Safe Bunk Formulas

Calculates how many classes can be safely skipped while staying ≥ 75%:

Safe Bunks = floor((Attended × 100 - Target × Held) / Target)
Must Attend = ceil((Target × Held - 100 × Attended) / (100 - Target))

If Safe Bunks is 3, skipping the next 3 classes will keep your attendance above the minimum bar.

02

Daily Punching

Fast logging through dashboard cards or the Timetable view:

  • Tap + Present if you were marked in class.
  • Tap + Bunk if you skipped or missed attendance.
  • Use the + / - buttons on cards to adjust manual counts for teacher corrections.
03

What-If Predictor

Before taking a leave or going to a college fest:

  • Select your subject in the Predictor tab.
  • Drag the Bunk Slider to see how many percent you will lose.
  • Check if the status remains green before making plans!
04

Medical & OD Leave

For authorized sports, events, or illness:

  • Click + Duty / Med Leave in the top dashboard banner.
  • Enter sanctioned hours approved by faculty.
  • Increments both attended & held count without penalizing your score.
